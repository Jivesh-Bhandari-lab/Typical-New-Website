About Us

Nurturing Excellence Since 2016

Holy Kids Convent School is a premier educational institution committed to delivering holistic education that empowers young learners with knowledge, values, and skills for tomorrow's world.

With two thriving branches — in Bidora Majhola, Uttarakhand (est. 2024) and Dhyanpur Nagla, Uttarakhand (est. 2016) — we proudly serve over 400 students, guided by a dedicated team of passionate educators and visionary leadership.
