Our Story

Nurturing Excellence Since 2016

Holy Kids Convent School was born from a simple yet powerful belief — that every child deserves access to quality education in a nurturing, safe environment that celebrates curiosity and builds character.

Founded in 2016 at Dhyanpur Nagla, our school has grown from a small but spirited institution into a thriving community of learners. In 2024, we expanded our vision by opening a second branch at Bidora Majhola, bringing our mission closer to even more families.

Today, we proudly serve over 400 students across both campuses — guided by passionate educators and the visionary leadership of Principal Pramod Chandra Upadhyay and Principal Krishna Chandra Upadhyay.

Our Journey

Key Milestones

A journey of growth, passion, and an unrelenting commitment to young learners.

2016 — The Beginning

Branch 1 Founded at Dhyanpur Nagla

Holy Kids Convent School opens its doors with a small but dedicated team, a handful of students, and a big dream for rural education.

2018 — Growing Strong

Reaching 100+ Students

Within two years, enrolment crosses the 100-student milestone as families from surrounding villages place their trust in Holy Kids.

2021 — Academic Excellence

Enhanced Curriculum & Activities

We introduce structured co-curricular programs, sports events, and cultural activities, shaping well-rounded young personalities.

2023 — Community Impact

300+ Students & Community Outreach

Enrolment surpasses 300 students. The school earns recognition as a cornerstone of the local educational community.

2024 — Expanding Our Vision

Branch 2 Opens at Bidora Majhola

A second campus is established to serve even more families, bringing the Holy Kids promise of quality education to a new community.

2025 — Today & Beyond

400+ Students, Brighter Futures

With over 400 students across two thriving branches, Holy Kids Convent continues to shape young minds with passion and purpose.
